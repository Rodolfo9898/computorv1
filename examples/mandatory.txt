# Mandatory examples (run with ./build/computor_v1 "<equation>")

5 * X^0 + 4 * X^1 - 9.3 * X^2 = 1 * X^0
5 * X^0 + 4 * X^1 = 4 * X^0
8 * X^0 - 6 * X^1 + 0 * X^2 - 5.6 * X^3 = 3 * X^0  # should report degree > 2 not solvable
6 * X^0 = 6 * X^0
10 * X^0 = 15 * X^0
1 * X^0 + 2 * X^1 + 5 * X^2 = 0
5 * X^0 + 13 * X^1 + 3 * X^2 = 1 * X^0 + 1 * X^1  # positive discriminant
6 * X^0 + 11 * X^1 + 5 * X^2 = 1 * X^0 + 1 * X^1  # zero discriminant
5 * X^0 + 3 * X^1 + 3 * X^2 = 1 * X^0 + 0 * X^1   # negative discriminant
